# Thinking, Fast and Slow
**Author:** Daniel Kahneman  
**Domain:** Cognitive Psychology & Decision Making  
**Classification:** Core Treatise  

## Summary
Kahneman explores the dual-system model of cognitive processing:
- **System 1:** Operates automatically, fast, with little or no effort, and no sense of voluntary control. Governed by associative memory, heuristics, emotional affect, and social conformity.
- **System 2:** Allocates attention to effortful mental operations, including complex computations, critical thinking, and conscious self-control.

### Key Behavioral Principles for Humanoid Simulation:
1. **Ego Depletion & Stress Overload:** When humanoids undergo cognitive load or sustained social stress, System 2 is depleted, defaulting to System 1 heuristics (loss aversion, peer herd following).
2. **Loss Aversion:** The psychological impact of losing status, peer trust, or belonging is measured as twice as potent as gaining equivalent status.
3. **Availability Heuristic:** Recent salient emotional events unduly dominate risk calculations.
