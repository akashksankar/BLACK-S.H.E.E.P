# Humanoid Social Contagion & Herd-Collapse Protocols
**Classification:** CLASSIFIED LABORATORY MANUAL (LEVEL-5)  
**Authors:** S.H.E.E.P. Cybernetics & Cognitive Architecture Group  
**Domain:** Complex Systems & Multi-Agent Social Simulation  

## Overview
When synthetic humanoid units are placed in dense social matrices (campus, council, workplace, residential wards), individual stress vectors do not remain localized. Under specific conditions, micro-anomalies cascade into macro-level herd panic or total conformity breakdown.

## Mathematical Formulation:
- **Contagion Velocity ($V_c$):** $V_c = \alpha \cdot \frac{S_{dep}}{R_{res}} \cdot \Delta P_{ext}$
  - where $S_{dep}$ is collective Social Dependency, $R_{res}$ is Emotional Resilience, and $\Delta P_{ext}$ is external stress delta.
- **Critical Threshold ($\tau_{herd}$):** If 3 or more high-status humanoid units display concurrent micro-freezes and eye-blocking within a 60-second window, adjacent subjects exhibit a 78% probability of mimicry within 300 seconds.

## Laboratory Intervention Protocols:
1. **Isolation Vector:** Inject high-frequency neutral stimuli to sever continuous conversational reinforcement.
2. **Confidence Inoculation:** Prime a designated high-stability subject (e.g. HX-091 Maya) with System 2 analytical prompts to anchor group deliberation.
