# The Prince
**Author:** Niccolò Machiavelli  
**Domain:** Political Philosophy & Strategic Calculus  
**Classification:** Core Treatise  

## Core Thesis
A ruler or decision-maker operating under realpolitik must balance moral appearance with pragmatic ruthlessness. The primary objective is stability, dominance, and neutralization of rival coalitions.

## Principles for S.H.E.E.P. Social Simulation:
1. **Fear vs. Affection:** It is vastly safer to be feared than loved when one must choose, as affection is easily broken when self-interest demands it, whereas fear is preserved by dread of punishment.
2. **The Fox and the Lion:** A subject must be a fox to recognize traps, and a lion to frighten wolves. Mere strength invites cunning subversion; mere cunning invites physical suppression.
3. **Pacing of Benefits and Injuries:**
   - Injuries should be inflicted all at once, so their taste is brief and resentment subsides faster.
   - Benefits should be conferred gradually, one by one, so that their taste may be savored longer.
4. **Alliances with Weak vs. Strong Neighbors:** Aligning with a peer stronger than oneself guarantees vassalage; patronizing a weaker peer creates enduring dependency.
