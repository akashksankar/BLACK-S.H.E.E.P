# Snakes in Suits: When Psychopaths Go to Work
**Authors:** Dr. Paul Babiak, Ph.D. & Dr. Robert D. Hare, Ph.D.  
**Domain:** Organizational Psychology & Psychopathy  
**Classification:** Core Treatise  

## Core Thesis
Corporate psychopathy thrives in environments undergoing rapid organizational transformation, ambiguity, or disruption. Psychopathic actors use charming veneers to gain entry, build influence networks, and systematically exploit systemic vulnerabilities while scapegoating conscientious colleagues.

## The Three-Phase Infiltration Model:
1. **Assessment Phase:** Rapid evaluation of key peer strengths, psychological insecurities, and power structures. Identifying who can be used, who can be charmed, and who must be neutralized.
2. **Manipulation Phase:** Disseminating conflicting narratives, gaslighting targets, cultivating patrons in upper management, and creating division among subordinates.
3. **Ascension / Confrontation Phase:** Stripping rivals of institutional credibility, claiming credit for collective successes, and abandoning loyal subordinates when utility expires.

## Behavioral Simulation Markers:
- High superficial charm coupled with low baseline autonomic stress (unflinching heart-rate telemetry under confrontation).
- Selective warmth: warmth exhibited exclusively toward decision-makers, cold indifference toward equals or subordinates.
- Absence of remorse following verified policy breaches.
