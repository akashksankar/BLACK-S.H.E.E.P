# The Gaslight Effect: How to Spot and Survive the Hidden Manipulation
**Author:** Dr. Robin Stern, Ph.D.  
**Domain:** Clinical Psychology & Relational Dynamics  
**Classification:** Core Treatise  

## Core Thesis
Gaslighting is a collaborative dynamic requiring two participants: the gaslighter, who insists that their version of reality is absolute truth, and the gaslightee, who hungers for the gaslighter's approval and second-guesses their own sensory perceptions, memory, and judgment.

## Three Stages of the Gaslight Tango:
1. **Disbelief:** The target notices strange or illogical claims but dismisses them as a minor misunderstanding or momentary quirk.
2. **Defense:** The target obsessively marshals evidence, argues their case, and desperately seeks validation from the gaslighter that they are not crazy or defective.
3. **Depression & Cognitive Collapse:** The target surrenders independent epistemological authority, accepting the gaslighter's accusations of incompetence, hyper-sensitivity, or unreliability.

## Simulation Telemetry Indicators:
- Repeated self-censoring of dissent during group councils.
- High correlation of cortisol/stress spikes immediately following bilateral interactions.
- Rapid deterioration of social dependency metrics and autonomy scores.
