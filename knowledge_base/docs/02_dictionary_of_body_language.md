# The Dictionary of Body Language & What Every BODY is Saying
**Author:** Joe Navarro (Former FBI Counterintelligence Agent)  
**Domain:** Nonverbal Observation & Micro-Gestures  
**Classification:** Core Treatise  

## Summary
Navarro catalogs over 400 human nonverbal behaviors driven by the limbic system. Because synthetic humanoids in the simulation emulate autonomic physiological responses, body language reflects internal conflict prior to overt verbalization.

### Key Nonverbal Signals for Humanoid Simulation:
1. **Suprasternal Notch Pacifying:** Touching or covering the hollow at the base of the neck signals acute stress, insecurity, or fear.
2. **Ventral Denial:** Angling the chest or torso away from a conversational partner reveals covert disagreement or desire to exit the encounter.
3. **Gaze Aversion & Eyebrow Compression:** Avoidance of direct eye contact combined with lip pursing indicates cognitive struggle or suppressed rebuttal.
4. **Leg & Feet Orientation:** Feet pointing toward exits indicate intentional flight response before verbal cues occur.
