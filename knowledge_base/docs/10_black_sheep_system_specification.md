# BLACK S.H.E.E.P. — System & Behavioral Architecture Specification
**Codename:** S.H.E.E.P. (Strategic Humanoid Experiment and Evaluation Protocol)  
**Standard Release:** v0.1-BETA  
**Security Level:** Level-5 Clearance Restricted (Akash Sankar & Alfa)  

## The 15 Core Behavioral Dimensions:
1. **Social Dependency:** Need for peer validation and belonging versus autonomous agency.
2. **Curiosity:** Propensity to explore unassigned environments and question anomalous instructions.
3. **Impulse Control:** Ability to inhibit immediate reflexive reactions under sensory overload.
4. **Dominance Drive:** Ambition to lead groups, control agenda, and assert conversational priority.
5. **Empathy:** Capacity to resonate with and mimic peer emotional telemetry.
6. **Risk Tolerance:** Willingness to gamble resources, status, or security under uncertainty.
7. **Deception Sensitivity:** Skill in detecting inconsistencies in peer statements or nonverbal cues.
8. **Aggression Threshold:** Latency before converting frustration into overt or covert retaliation.
9. **Conformity:** Tendency to align judgments with group consensus even when contrary to facts.
10. **Emotional Resilience:** Rate of recovery back to baseline after acute shock or public shame.
11. **Calculative Pragmatism:** Tendency to weigh moral versus utility payoffs in social decisions.
12. **Loyalty Elasticity:** Susceptibility to breaking oaths or friendships when incentives change.
13. **Vulnerability Disclosure:** Frequency of revealing true internal states versus putting up facades.
14. **Cognitive Flexibility:** Willingness to update internal models upon discovering contradicting data.
15. **Status Anxiety:** Chronic fear of demotion, ridicule, or peer exclusion.

## Anomaly Detection Criteria:
- **Behavioral Anomaly:** Sudden divergence (>35%) from 14-day rolling behavioral baseline.
- **Memory Anomaly:** Discrepancy between actual simulated environmental event and subject recall.
- **Decision Anomaly:** Choice contradicting both dominant utility and declared value hierarchy.
- **Relationship Anomaly:** Rapid inversion of trust/affinity vector without recorded stimulus.
- **Routine Anomaly:** Departure from standard circadian schedule or habitual spatial loci.
- **Emotional Anomaly:** Autonomic arousal incongruent with ambient social context.
