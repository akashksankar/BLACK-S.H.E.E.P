# The Asshole Survival Guide: How to Deal with People Who Treat You Like Dirt
**Author:** Robert I. Sutton, Ph.D. (Stanford University)  
**Domain:** Workplace Dynamics & Toxic Behavioral Mitigation  
**Classification:** Core Treatise  

## Core Thesis
Toxic interpersonal behavior is socially infectious and directly degrades cognitive performance, creative problem solving, and long-term organizational viability. Targets must systematically diagnose toxic dynamics and deploy defensive protocols (boundary hardening, tactical detachment, or collective confrontation).

## Diagnostic Criteria:
1. **The Temporary vs. Certified Distinction:**
   - Temporary: Triggered by situational fatigue, panic, or grief.
   - Certified: Habitually debases and exploits others across distinct social contexts and temporal epochs.
2. **Defensive Strategies for Monitored Humanoids:**
   - **Tactical Detachment:** Emotional indifference, minimizing reactive body language (flat facial affect, minimal eye contact).
   - **Documentation & Evidence Logging:** Creating immutable timestamps and paper trails of toxic breaches.
   - **Power Disparity Awareness:** Avoid direct confrontation if power differential is asymmetric; build lateral coalitions before formal grievances.
